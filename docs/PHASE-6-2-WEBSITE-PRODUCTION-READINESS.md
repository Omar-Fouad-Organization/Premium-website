## Monitoring, incident response and privacy policy proposal

Existing structured DB lifecycle/command/scan audits cover issuance, claims, redemption, revocation and reconciliation; failed Laravel jobs and scheduler heartbeat provide building blocks, not a configured monitoring service. No alert was provisioned/tested. Available local Laravel logs (ERP2,397,227bytes, website294,713bytes) were scanned for exact known synthetic cryptographic keys and passwords: zero matches; [scoped log review](phase62-evidence/log-review.json). This is not broad PII, all unknown tokens or production/proxy logging certification. Logs must use command/invitation/file IDs, operation/event and sanitized failure codes, never request bodies, passwords, tokens, cookie/header values, encryption keys, private bytes or full recipient details. Provider exception text is reduced to classified codes. Reverse proxy/central log redaction, access restrictions, alert destinations and tamper resistance remain staging gates.

Proposed alerts (not active): repeated login/replay rejection, unknown/bounced mail, queue oldest age>5min, scan unavailable/age/signature staleness, persistent gateway failures, any repeated deadlock/slot conflict spike, missed scheduler heartbeat and unexpected privilege changes. Security/on-call owns containment; business owner decides representative restoration. Include environment+application+request/command ID in correlation, not user-entered secrets.

Incident runbooks: compromised account→disable at ERP authority, revoke website devices/auth versions, stop pending effects and preserve evidence; leaked invitation→scoped revoke, cancel/wipe intent, verify representative then fresh issue; compromised SMTP credentials→pause delivery/flags, revoke provider credential through authorized owner, reconcile unknowns before reissue; scanner outage/suspicious PDF→keep quarantine and deny release, isolate object, restore approved engine/signatures then authorized rescan; unauthorized data access→contain actor/key scope, preserve sanitized audits/backups, notify security/privacy owners for assessment; gateway outage→retain pending commands/receipts, fail closed and reconcile rather than duplicate; accidental revocation→staff-reviewed explicit regrant of current authority, never restore stale sessions or bulk-update memberships.
